Hola, soy Pat.

I'm a wife, a mom in a beautifully blended family of seven, a woman of faith, and a tech girl who loves turning everyday problems into useful little tools.

Some days I'm deep in a project. Other days I'm figuring out dinner, the budget, or what our family needs next. Most days, it's a little bit of everything.
